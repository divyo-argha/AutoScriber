Interviewer: Can you tell me about your experience using mobile apps for daily tasks?

Participant 1: Well, I use many apps every day. But sometimes I find them very confusing. The buttons are too small and I can't read the text properly. আমার চোখ ভালো না, তাই বড় লেখা দরকার।

Interviewer: What specific problems do you face?

Participant 1: The main problem is navigation. When I open an app, I don't know where to go. Too many options, too many menus. আমি হারিয়ে যাই। And the colors are not good - everything looks the same.

Interviewer: Have you tried using accessibility features?

Participant 1: Yes, I tried the voice feature. But it doesn't work well with Bangla. It only speaks English properly. আমার ভাষায় কথা বলে না। This is very frustrating for me.
