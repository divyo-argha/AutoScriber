Features

Everything you need to transcribe

Built specifically for Bangla language researchers, journalists, and practitioners.

Bangla & English

Full support for Bangla transcription with seamless code-switching between Bangla and English in the same audio.

Speaker Diarization

Automatically identifies and labels different speakers throughout the recording. Know who said what, when.

Word-Level Timestamps

Every word is timestamped. Click any word in the transcript to jump to that exact moment in the audio.

6 Export Formats

Export your transcripts as TXT, SRT, JSON, DOCX, PDF, or ZIP. Use them anywhere, in any workflow.

Private by Design

Your audio and transcripts are stored only on your machine. The only outbound requests are AI API calls — no telemetry, no tracking.

6 Gemini AI Models

Choose from Gemini 2.5 Pro, 2.5 Flash, 2.5 Flash-Lite, 2.0 Flash, 1.5 Flash, or 1.5 Pro — each tuned for different speed and accuracy needs.

Vertex AI Enterprise

Use Google Cloud Vertex AI with service account credentials for secure, high-scale enterprise inference.

Job Control

Pause, resume, and cancel transcription jobs in real time — full control while processing.

Batch Queueing

Queue multiple files simultaneously with individual progress tracking for every job.

Browser Recording

Record high-quality audio directly via your microphone — no file uploads required.

About

Built for Bangla.
Powered by AI.

autoScriber (স্বয়ংলিপি — "self-writing") was built to solve a real problem: there were no good tools for transcribing Bangla audio with the accuracy and features that researchers and journalists actually need.

It uses Google's Gemini AI models — through the Gemini API (Google AI Studio) or Google Cloud Vertex AI — to deliver accurate Bangla transcription with automatic speaker identification and word-level synchronization — all running as a native desktop app with your data staying entirely on your machine.

Built with care for Bangla language researchers, oral historians, journalists, and anyone working with spoken Bangla content.
