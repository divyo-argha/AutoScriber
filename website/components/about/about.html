About

Built for Bangla.
Powered by AI.

autoScriber, meaning "auto-transcriber" in Bangla (স্বয়ংলিপি), was built to solve a specific challenge: the lack of accurate tools for transcribing Bangla audio with the detailed formatting that researchers, oral historians, and journalists require.

The application securely routes audio to Google Gemini AI models through either Google AI Studio or Google Cloud Vertex AI. This approach delivers precise Bangla transcripts alongside automatic speaker identification and word-level synchronization, all while keeping your data saved locally.

Every feature is optimized to respect privacy, run efficiently on standard consumer hardware, and facilitate quick exports for professional analysis.
