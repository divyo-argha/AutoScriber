How It Works

From audio to transcript in four steps

The same privacy-first pipeline runs in the desktop app and the browser app — no backend, no cloud storage.

01

Add audio

Upload MP3, WAV, M4A, WEBM, OGG, or FLAC files — or record directly from your microphone.

02

Split in-browser

WebAssembly FFmpeg chunks long recordings locally with overlap — nothing leaves your machine.

03

Transcribe with Gemini

Your chosen model transcribes every chunk with speaker diarization and word-level timestamps.

04

Review & export

Explore the interactive viewer with clickable timestamps and export to TXT, SRT, JSON, DOCX, PDF, or ZIP.

No account. No cloud storage. The only outbound request is the AI API call to the model you choose.
