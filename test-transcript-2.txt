Interviewer: How do you feel about the current mobile banking apps?

Participant 2: Mobile banking is necessary now, but the apps are not user-friendly. প্রথমবার ব্যবহার করতে অনেক সমস্যা হয়েছিল। The registration process was very complicated.

Interviewer: What made it complicated?

Participant 2: Too many steps. They ask for so much information. And the verification process takes forever. আমাকে তিনবার চেষ্টা করতে হয়েছিল। Also, the error messages don't explain what went wrong.

Interviewer: Do you feel secure using these apps?

Participant 2: Not really. I'm always worried about security. The app doesn't show me clearly if my transaction was successful or not. কখনো কখনো টাকা কেটে নেয় কিন্তু confirmation আসে না। This creates a lot of anxiety.

Interviewer: What improvements would you suggest?

Participant 2: Make it simpler. Less steps. Clear messages in Bangla. And better security indicators so I know my money is safe. বাংলায় সব কিছু থাকলে ভালো হয়।
